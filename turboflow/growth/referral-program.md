# Referral Program

TurboFlow’s Referral Program rewards both inviters and invitees for bringing new participants to the platform.

Share your referral link or code with others. When an eligible referred user joins and trades on TurboFlow, referral rewards are distributed according to the applicable product and reward structure.

### How It Works

1. Share your TurboFlow referral link or referral code.
2. Your invitee signs up using your referral.
3. When the invitee completes eligible trading activity, both the inviter and invitee may receive referral rewards.

Referral rewards vary by product.

### Why It Matters

The Referral Program gives users a simple way to grow the TurboFlow community while earning rewards from eligible activity generated by the users they invite.

Both inviters and invitees can benefit, creating a more participation-driven growth model across the platform.

### Important Notice

Referral eligibility, reward rates, qualifying activity, and related conditions are subject to TurboFlow’s applicable program rules and may change over time.

Users should always refer to the latest product interface and official TurboFlow announcements for current referral terms and reward rates.
